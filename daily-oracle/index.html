How it works

Open it with your coffee. Done in a minute.

No setup and no account. The day's card is waiting, face down, along with the moon for your date and time zone.

02

FAQ

Questions

Does everyone get the same tarot card of the day?

Yes. Everyone who opens Daily Oracle on the same local date draws the same card, so you can share it and reflect on the same thing as a friend. The cards follow a fixed shuffle for each year, so a card comes back about every 78 days, and never sooner than 40.

How accurate is the moon phase?

The app calculates the Moon's position on your phone with the formulas in Jean Meeus's Astronomical Algorithms. Checked against all 99 new moons, full moons and quarters the U.S. Naval Observatory lists for 2026 and 2027, every phase time is within a few minutes. The moon sign is the Moon's tropical zodiac sign, with the time it enters the next one.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The cards, quotes, actions and moon calculations are all on your phone. Ads need a connection; without one the app simply shows none, and the 3-card spread opens anyway.

Is Daily Oracle free?

Yes, every feature is free. The app shows a small banner ad, a short optional video before the 3-card spread, and at most one full-screen ad a day after you share. There are no purchases and no subscription.

Is it fortune telling?

No. Daily Oracle uses tarot as a prompt for reflection, the way you might use a journaling card. It doesn't predict the future and isn't advice of any kind.

What data does the app collect?

Your card history, actions and reminder time stay on your phone and are never sent to us. There is no account. The ads come from Google AdMob, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. In the EU, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choices in Settings.

Where do the cards and quotes come from?

The card art is scans of the original 1909 Rider-Waite-Smith deck illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith, which is in the public domain. The quotes are George Long's public-domain translations of Marcus Aurelius's Meditations and Epictetus's Enchiridion and Discourses. The card reflections and daily actions were written for the app.

Will it remind me every day?

Only if you want it to. You can turn on one daily reminder at a time you choose. It is a local notification scheduled on your phone, it never gives away the card, and it skips the day once you have drawn.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Daily Oracle is available for Android 8.0 and later.
