Paper privacy policy

Paper: Document Annotator, by Kan Canada Trading Corp. Effective 27 November 2026.

The short version

Paper does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Your files and your notes stay on your phone.

Your files

When you open a file, Paper makes its own copy on your phone so you can write on it. The original file is never changed. Converting Word, Excel, PowerPoint and EPUB files to pages happens on your phone, offline. Your copies, ink and signature are stored only in Paper's private storage on your device. Removing a file from Paper, or uninstalling Paper, deletes them. Paper excludes this data from Android cloud backups.

Sharing

A file leaves your phone only when you choose to share or save an exported PDF, and only to the app or place you pick.

Purchases

Paper Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. Paper only receives a confirmation that you own Pro; it never sees your payment details.

Permissions

Paper asks for no runtime permissions and does not have the internet permission. Files reach Paper only through the system file picker, "Open with" and Share.

Children

Paper does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions: asoni@kancanada.com.
