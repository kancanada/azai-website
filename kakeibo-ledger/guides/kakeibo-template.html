Updated 15 November 2026 · 6 min read
Short answerThis page is the template. Print it (the button below prints just the worksheets) or copy the plain-text version into a notes app or document. It has four parts: a monthly plan (income, fixed costs, savings goal, spending money), a daily ledger with columns for needs, wants, culture and unexpected, weekly totals, and the four month-end questions. There's also a spreadsheet layout with formulas.

We make Kakeibo Ledger, an Android app that does the same job as this template; we mention it at the end. The template is free to print and copy for personal use, with no sign-up.

A kakeibo template doesn't need to be fancy. It needs a place to plan the month, a place to write each expense with its category, and a place to look back. Here are all three, in a format you can print on plain paper, copy into a notes app, or rebuild in a spreadsheet.

If kakeibo is new to you, read what is kakeibo first. It takes five minutes and makes the template make sense.

Spreadsheet version

If you'd rather have the totals done for you, set up a sheet with these columns in row 1: Date (A), What (B), Box (C), Amount (D). Enter one expense per row and type N, W, C or U in the Box column. Then add a small summary block:

        F             G
1   Needs         =SUMIF(C:C,"N",D:D)
2   Wants         =SUMIF(C:C,"W",D:D)
3   Culture       =SUMIF(C:C,"C",D:D)
4   Unexpected    =SUMIF(C:C,"U",D:D)
5   Total         =SUM(G1:G4)

These formulas work in Google Sheets, Excel, LibreOffice Calc and Apple Numbers. Use one tab per month and keep the plan numbers at the top of each tab. Resist the urge to add sub-categories; four boxes is the point.

How to fill it in: a short example

Say your take-home pay is $3,200, fixed costs are $1,750 and your savings goal is $300. Spending money is $1,150. You might budget $575 for needs, $290 for wants, $115 for culture and $170 for unexpected.

On the 3rd you buy groceries for $86.40 (N), a coffee for $5.25 (W) and a paperback for $18 (C). On the 9th your bike needs a $45 repair (U). Each goes on its own line, in its own column. On Sunday you add the columns up: if wants is already at $140 after one week, you know where to ease off.

At month end, you copy the totals into Part 4, answer the questions, and write one change. That change goes at the top of next month's plan so you see it on day one.

Tips that make a paper ledger stick

  • Keep it where you pay. In your bag, or on your phone. A ledger left at home gets filled in from memory, and memory forgets coffees.
  • Record refunds as negative amounts in the same box as the original purchase.
  • Split mixed receipts. A supermarket trip with groceries and a bottle of wine can be two lines: needs and wants.
  • Don't record fixed costs in the ledger. They're already in the plan. The ledger is for day-to-day spending.
  • Check your bank statement weekly for card purchases you forgot to write down. See how to track expenses by hand for more on this.

Questions

Is this kakeibo template free?

Yes. You can print it or copy it for personal use without signing up. The print button prints only the worksheets: the plan, the ledger, the weekly totals and the reflection.

What should a kakeibo template include?

A monthly plan with income, fixed costs, a savings goal and spending money; a daily ledger with columns for needs, wants, culture and unexpected; weekly totals; and the four month-end questions.

Should fixed costs go in the kakeibo ledger?

Usually not. Fixed costs such as rent and insurance are taken out in the monthly plan, so the ledger only tracks day-to-day spending. That keeps the ledger short and focused on the money you can actually change.

Can I use the kakeibo template in a spreadsheet?

Yes. Use columns for date, what, box and amount, type N, W, C or U for the box, and add totals with SUMIF formulas. The formulas on this page work in Google Sheets, Excel, LibreOffice Calc and Numbers.

How many ledger pages do I need a month?

Most people need two or three printed ledger pages a month. If you make many small purchases, print more, or switch to a spreadsheet or app so totals are added for you.
