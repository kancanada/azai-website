Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 (version 1.0)

The short version

Kakeibo Ledger collects nothing. It doesn't have Android's internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere. There is no account, no analytics and no ads, and we run no servers. Your plans, expenses, notes and reflections stay on your phone.

Who we are

Kakeibo Ledger: Budget Journal is published by Kan Canada Trading Corp., a Canadian company based in British Columbia, under its AZAI brand. Contact: asoni@kancanada.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your monthly plans: income, fixed costs, savings goal and the split between the four categories.
  • Your expenses, with their amount, category, date and optional note.
  • Your month-end reflections and weekly check-in notes.
  • Your settings, such as currency, theme, reminder and app-lock choices.

All of this is kept in a database in the app's private storage. None of it is sent to us or any third party. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app, and uninstalling the app deletes everything.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you choose to export it yourself:

  • Backup file. When you tap "Save encrypted backup", the app encrypts your ledger with a passphrase you choose (AES-256-GCM, with the key derived by scrypt) and opens Android's share sheet. You decide where the file goes, for example Google Drive or email. We never see the file or the passphrase, and we can't recover a forgotten passphrase.
  • CSV export (Pro). The same, as an unencrypted spreadsheet of your expenses.

What happens to an exported file after that is up to you and the app you send it to.

Purchases

Kakeibo Ledger Pro is sold through Google Play, and Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. The app only asks the Google Play app on your phone whether Pro has been bought; it receives no payment or personal details, and we don't either.

No analytics, no ads, no selling

The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK. We don't sell personal information, and we don't receive any data from the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): the Sunday check-in reminder. It's scheduled on your phone; no server sends it.
  • Run at startup: lets Android restore that reminder after your phone restarts.
  • Biometrics (Pro, optional): app lock uses Android's own fingerprint, face or PIN check. The app never sees your biometric data.
  • Google Play billing: to buy and restore Pro.
  • Vibration: a short tap when you save an entry.

The app doesn't request internet, network state, location, contacts, storage, camera, microphone or the advertising ID.

Children

Kakeibo Ledger isn't directed at children under 13 and is listed for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

Your rights

Under PIPEDA (Canada), GDPR (EU/EEA) and similar laws elsewhere, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. We don't hold any personal data about you: everything the app stores is on your phone, where you can edit or delete it, or remove it all by uninstalling. Questions: asoni@kancanada.com.
