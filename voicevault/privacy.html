Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The short version

VoiceVault has no servers, no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics. Your recordings and transcripts stay on your phone. If you connect Google Drive, copies go to your Drive, and nowhere else.

Who we are

VoiceVault is published by Kan Canada Trading Corp., a Canadian company based in British Columbia. Contact: asoni@kancanada.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Audio recordings you make, saved in Recordings/VoiceVault (or a folder you choose).
  • Transcripts of those recordings, saved in Documents/VoiceVault (or a folder you choose).
  • Settings, such as your language and save folder.

We never receive any of this. Uninstalling the app removes its settings; your saved files stay until you delete them.

How transcription works

VoiceVault turns speech into text using the speech recognition service built into your phone (Speech Services by Google). By default it runs on-device: audio is processed on your phone and is not sent over the internet.

Settings include an optional, off-by-default switch, “Fall back to Google online”. If you turn it on and on-device recognition fails, audio is sent to Google’s online speech service while it’s in use, under Google’s Privacy Policy.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive, VoiceVault asks Google for the drive.file permission. This lets the app create and update only the files it makes itself, in a “VoiceVault” folder in your Drive. It cannot see, read or change anything else in your Drive.

  • What is uploaded: each recording’s transcript, and the audio if you leave “Upload audio too” on.
  • Where it goes: directly from your phone to your own Google Drive. It never passes through our servers.
  • What we see: nothing. We have no access to your Drive or your Google account.
  • You can disconnect any time in Settings, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

VoiceVault’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

What VoiceVault does NOT collect

  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising IDs, device IDs or location.
  • No contacts, photos or other files.

Permissions VoiceVault asks for

  • Microphone: to record, only while you are recording.
  • Notifications: to show that recording is in progress, with a Stop button.
  • Internet: used only to upload to your Google Drive, if you connect it.

Children

VoiceVault is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. Your recordings are yours: delete them on your phone or in your Drive at any time. Questions: asoni@kancanada.com.
