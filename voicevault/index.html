Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After the first setup, when VoiceVault downloads its AI to your phone over Wi-Fi, recording, transcripts, summaries and Ask all work in airplane mode.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 13 and newer. Summaries are fastest on recent phones.

Can it record online meetings?

VoiceVault records what your phone's microphone hears, so it's made for meetings in the room and calls on speaker. It doesn't join Zoom or Teams as a bot.

Can people see that I'm recording?

VoiceVault shows a recording notification, and Android shows the microphone indicator. Always tell people when you're recording, and follow the consent rules where you are.

What happens after the 30 days?

To keep recording, unlock VoiceVault with one payment. If you don't, everything you've already recorded stays readable on your phone.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. VoiceVault is Android only for now.
