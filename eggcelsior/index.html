How to play

Tap. Toss. Land a PERFECT.

Tap anywhere and the egg flips straight up. The next basket keeps sliding, so timing is everything. Land in the middle for a PERFECT; land three in a row and your egg catches fire.

Miss and the egg cracks, right on your screen. You have three eggs per run. A near miss drops into slow motion, so you can watch it wobble on the rim.

FAQ

Questions

How do you play Eggcelsior?

Your egg sits in a basket and the baskets above slide side to side. Tap anywhere to toss the egg straight up. Time it so it drops into the next basket. Land in the middle for a PERFECT; chain them for a combo. Miss and the egg cracks. You have three eggs per run.

Is Eggcelsior free?

Yes. Every mode and 8 of the 12 egg skins are free. The game shows an occasional full-screen ad between runs (never during one) and offers an optional video to continue after you crack. A one-time US$2.99 purchase removes the ads between runs, and the Royal Egg Pack adds four cosmetic skins for US$1.99. There is no subscription.

What is the Daily Tower?

Each day there is one tower that is identical for every player in the world, so you can compare heights with friends. It changes at midnight UTC.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The game runs completely on your phone. Ads need a connection; without one the game simply shows none.

What data does the game collect?

Your scores, stars, skins and settings stay on your phone. There is no account. The ads come from Google AdMob, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. In the EU, UK and Switzerland the game asks for consent first.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Eggcelsior is available for Android.
