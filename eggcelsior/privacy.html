Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 (version 1.0)

The short version

Eggcelsior has no account and no analytics of its own, and we run no servers. Your scores, stars and skins stay on your phone. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ad SDK collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Who we are

Eggcelsior: Egg Toss Climb is published by Kan Canada Trading Corp., a Canadian company based in British Columbia, under its AZAI brand. Contact: asoni@kancanada.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your best height, today's Daily Tower best and your daily streak.
  • The stars you've earned and the egg skins you've unlocked.
  • Your settings: sound, music and vibration.
  • Whether you've bought Remove Ads or the Royal Egg Pack, as reported by Google Play.

None of this is sent to us or any third party, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to continue a run, and an occasional full-screen ad between runs, never during one. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic and crash information.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We don't receive this data. The game asks Google for ads rated PG or lower.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or in a US state whose privacy law requires it, the game asks for your consent or choices before ads are personalised. Where applicable you can change your choices in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Buying Remove Ads stops the full-screen ads between runs. The optional "watch a video to continue" offer stays available, but only plays if you choose it.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Ads and the Royal Egg Pack) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google Play only tells the game which items you own so it can unlock them.

No analytics, no selling

The game has no analytics SDK of its own. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't receive any data from the game ourselves.

Share images

When you tap Share, an image of your height is drawn on your phone. On Android 10 and later it's saved to your Pictures so it can be handed to the app you choose through Android's share sheet. Eggcelsior doesn't upload it anywhere.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load ads and by Google Play Billing for purchases. The game itself works offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.
  • Billing: lets Google Play handle in-app purchases.
  • Vibration: short haptic taps when you toss, land and crack. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

Eggcelsior isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will be noted in the game's release notes.

Your rights

Under PIPEDA (Canada), GDPR (EU/EEA) and similar laws elsewhere, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. We don't hold any personal data about you. For data processed by Google for ads, use the choices above or Google's own tools. Questions: asoni@kancanada.com.
