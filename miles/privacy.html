Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 (version 1.2, for app version 1.2.0 and later)

The short version

AZAI Miles records your drives so you can keep a mileage log. Your trips, places, purposes and settings are stored on your phone. The app has no account, no analytics and no ads, and we run no servers, so we never receive your trips.

The app goes online for four things: to show the street name where each trip started and ended, to load map images for your trips and places, to look up a place or address when you search for one, and to let Google Play handle the Pro purchase. Each is described below. Your trip log itself is never uploaded.

Location

AZAI Miles collects location data to record your trips automatically, even when the app is closed or not in use. This is the app's core feature: most drives start while the app is closed, so it needs location "all the time" to log them.

  • Location is used only to measure each trip's distance and its start and end points, to show the street name of where each trip started and ended, to recognise places you visit often, and for the map and address features below.
  • While a trip is being recorded, Android shows a "Recording a trip" notification.
  • The GPS points of your trips are stored on your phone. We never receive them, and we never sell or share your location.
  • You can turn automatic tracking off at any time in the app's Settings, or remove the location permission in Android Settings.

When the app goes online

  • Street names. When a trip ends, the app sends the exact spots where it started and stopped (their coordinates) to your phone's built-in address service (Android's Geocoder, provided by Google Play services on most Android phones) to get the street and town, such as "Main Street, Springfield". This happens automatically, also when the app is closed, and for older stops the app hasn't looked up yet while the app is open. Only the coordinates are sent: no trip, name, account or ID. Google handles these requests under the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn this off in Settings → Show street names; streets already looked up stay on your phone.
  • Maps. The app shows maps of your trips and places: on a trip, on the Places screen, in small map pictures next to each trip and place in your lists, and when you pick a spot on the map. It loads the map images for those areas from OpenFreeMap, a free map service. Like any website, OpenFreeMap receives your phone's IP address and which map areas are being shown, which can reveal the approximate area of your trips and places. Your trips themselves are not sent. If you're offline, the app draws the route from your phone's GPS points instead. When you move the pin on a map, the street under the pin is looked up with your phone's address service, as for street names.
  • Place and address search. When you type a place or address in a search box (for example "Costco"), the app sends the text you typed and your approximate location (your last known position, rounded to about 1 km) to two services, so it can show results near you: Photon, a place search built on OpenStreetMap data and run by Komoot, and your phone's built-in address service (Android's Geocoder, provided by Google Play services on most Android phones), which receives a rough area around that position. Like any website, Photon also receives your phone's IP address. No account, name or ID is sent. Komoot handles these requests under the Komoot Privacy Policy, and Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Search results are © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • "Name where I am now", "Use the street name" and "Find its address". When you ask the app to name a spot or find its address, that spot's coordinates are looked up by your phone's address service (Google, as above). The search also looks up the name of your town this way, to find businesses near you.
  • Email to your accountant. If you use "Email to my accountant", the app hands the log files and the address you typed to your own email app, and your email app sends them. The app itself sends nothing, and we don't see the email.
  • Pro purchase. Handled by Google Play (see Purchases).

These requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We don't operate these services and receive nothing from them.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your trips: times, distances, the GPS points of each route, start and end places, business or personal, purposes, parking and tolls, and vehicle.
  • Places you name or add, their street names and addresses, and any "always business", route or working-hours rules you set.
  • Your vehicles, licence plates and odometer readings, your country and tax-rate settings, the optional report details you put on your log (your name, business or employer, employee ID) and your accountant's email address if you add it.
  • Small map pictures of your trips and places, kept so lists load quickly.
  • Whether you've bought Pro, as reported by Google Play.

We have no copy of any of this. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and Settings → Delete all data deletes it at any time.

Spoken purposes

When you hold the microphone button, Android's on-device speech recognizer turns what you say into text on your phone. Only the text is saved, as the trip's purpose. The audio is not kept and is not sent to us. The microphone is never used in the background.

Physical activity

The app uses Android's activity recognition (provided by Google Play services on your phone) to notice when you start and stop driving, so it can begin and end a trip. The signal is read on your phone and is used only to start and stop trip recording.

Notifications

When a trip ends, the app can show a "Trip logged" notification so you can mark it business or personal without opening the app. It is created on your phone and can be turned off in Settings → Ask after each trip.

Reports and backups

Mileage reports (PDF or CSV) and backup files are created on your phone and go only where you choose to save or share them, for example your own Google Drive or an email to your accountant. Once you share a file, the service you share it with handles it under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

The Pro unlock is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play, using Google's Play Billing library. We never see your payment details; Google Play tells the app whether you own Pro and when it was bought. Google handles purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy.

No analytics, no ads, no selling

The app has no analytics or advertising SDK, no advertising ID, and shows no ads. We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't receive any data from the app.

Permissions

  • Physical activity: to detect when a drive starts and ends.
  • Location (precise, and all the time): to record trips, including when the app is closed.
  • Foreground service (location): to keep recording a drive, with a visible notification, until it ends.
  • Notifications: the "Recording a trip" and "Trip logged" notifications, the evening "trips to sort" reminder and the monthly "your log is ready" reminder.
  • Run at startup: to restart drive detection after your phone restarts.
  • Microphone: only while you hold the button to speak a trip's purpose.
  • Internet and network state: to look up street names and addresses, load map images, and tell you when you're offline.
  • Billing: lets Google Play handle the Pro purchase.
