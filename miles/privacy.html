Privacy Policy

The short version

AZAI Miles records your drives so you can keep a mileage log. Your location, trips, places and spoken notes are stored only on your phone. The app has no account, no analytics and no ads, we run no servers, and the app doesn't even have permission to use the internet. We never see your data.

Who we are

AZAI Miles: Mileage Tracker is published by Kan Canada Trading Corp., a Canadian company based in British Columbia, under its AZAI brand. Contact: asoni@kancanada.com.

Location

AZAI Miles collects location data to record your trips automatically, even when the app is closed or not in use. This is the app's core feature: most drives start while the app is closed, so it needs location "all the time" to log them.

  • Location is used only to measure each trip's distance and its start and end points, and to recognise places you visit often.
  • While a trip is being recorded, Android shows a "Recording a trip" notification.
  • Location is stored only on your phone. It is never sent to us, sold or shared with anyone.
  • Routes are drawn from your phone's own GPS points. No map is loaded, and coordinates are not turned into street addresses, so no location is sent to a map or address service.
  • You can turn automatic tracking off at any time in the app's Settings, or remove the location permission in Android Settings.

What stays on your phone

  • Your trips: times, distances, the GPS points of each route, start and end places, business or personal, purposes and vehicle.
  • Places you name, and any "always business" rules you set.
  • Your vehicles and odometer readings, your country and tax-rate settings, and the optional name you put on reports.
  • Whether you've bought Pro, as reported by Google Play.

None of this is sent to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and Settings → Delete all data deletes it at any time.

Spoken purposes

When you hold the microphone button, Android's on-device speech recognizer turns what you say into text on your phone. Only the text is saved, as the trip's purpose. The audio is not stored and is not sent to us. The microphone is never used in the background.

Physical activity

The app uses Android's activity recognition (provided by Google Play services on your phone) to notice when you start and stop driving, so it can begin and end a trip. The result is used only to start and stop trip recording.

Reports and backups

Mileage reports (PDF or CSV) and backup files are created on your phone and go only where you choose to save or share them, for example your own Google Drive or an email to your accountant. Once you share a file, the service you share it with handles it under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

The Pro unlock is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google Play only tells the app whether you own Pro.

No analytics, no ads, no selling

The app has no analytics or advertising SDK and shows no ads. We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't receive any data from the app.

Permissions

  • Physical activity: to detect when a drive starts and ends.
  • Location (precise, and all the time): to record trips, including when the app is closed.
  • Foreground service (location): to keep recording a drive, with a visible notification, until it ends.
  • Notifications: the "Recording a trip" notification, the evening "trips to sort" reminder and the monthly "your log is ready" reminder.
  • Run at startup: to restart drive detection after your phone restarts.
  • Microphone: only while you hold the button to speak a trip's purpose.
  • Billing: lets Google Play handle the Pro purchase.

The app does not request internet access.

Children

AZAI Miles is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
