How it works

Three steps. No setup, no sign-up.

Flip to Focus uses your phone's motion sensor to tell when the screen faces the desk, so the only thing you have to do is put it down.

01

Pick a session length

Anywhere from 15 to 120 minutes, or tap a preset. Tag it Study, Work or Writing if you like.

02

Flip your phone face down

The timer starts the moment the screen faces the desk. The display switches off and notifications go quiet.

03

Pick it up and it pauses

Lift the phone and the clock stops until you turn it back over. With Strict Mode on, more than 10 seconds ends the session.

FAQ

Questions

How does a flip study timer work?

Set a session from 15 to 120 minutes and turn your phone face down on the desk. Flip to Focus uses the motion sensor to detect that the screen faces down and starts the timer. If you pick the phone up, the session pauses until you turn it back over.

Is Flip to Focus free?

Every feature is free for 30 days. After that, a single one-time purchase keeps you starting new sessions. There is no subscription and no ads. Your history and heatmap stay free either way.

Does Flip to Focus collect my data?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no server. Your sessions are stored only on your phone, and you can export them as a CSV file whenever you want.

Does it work with a phone case or on an uneven desk?

Yes. Face-down detection allows up to 25 degrees of tilt, and a short hold of about 1.5 seconds means bumping the desk does not count as picking the phone up.

Will notifications still buzz while I study?

Not if you allow Do Not Disturb access. Flip to Focus turns Do Not Disturb on when a session starts and restores your previous setting when it ends.

Does it drain the battery?

The screen switches off while the phone lies face down, so a session uses far less battery than a timer that keeps the display on.

Can it help if I have ADHD?

Many people find that putting the phone face down and out of sight makes it easier to stay on task, and Flip to Focus makes that a routine you can track. It is a productivity tool, not a medical treatment.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Flip to Focus is available for Android 8.0 and later.
