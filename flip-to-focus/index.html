What it does

Flip to start

Set 15 to 120 minutes and turn your phone over. The screen goes dark and the timer runs until you're done.

Silence, automatically

Do Not Disturb turns on for the session and back off when it ends. Strict Mode ends the session if you pick the phone up.

Streaks you can see

A year-long heatmap of your focused minutes. Everything stays on your phone, and you can export it any time.

Pricing
